# Architecture Templates

Use these templates to make review requests and engagement decisions concrete. Copy customer-specific records into an approved workspace and share only appropriately classified evidence.

| Template | Use |
|---|---|
| [Design review request](design-review-request.md) | Submit a design with its context, decisions, and review evidence |
| [Architecture leadership engagement](architecture-leadership-engagement.md) | Capture owners, customer baseline, assumptions, options, validation, readiness, and outcome reviews |

See the [practical architecture leadership guide](../docs/practical-architecture-leadership.md), [architecture review process](../docs/architecture-review-process.md), and [ADR template](../adr/0000-template.md) for the surrounding practices.
